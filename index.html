Slots sheet, one row per advising slot:

ColumnNeededExample
ProfessorYesMust match a name on the Faculty sheet
DayYesMonday or Mon. Mon/Wed makes one slot per day.
Start, EndYes12:00 PM and 1:30 PM. A single Time column like 12pm-1:30pm also works.
ModeYesIn Person, Online, or In Person or Online
Room, Zoom Link, Booking Link, NotesOnly if differentLeave blank to use the advisor's details from the Faculty sheet

Column names are flexible (Name, Faculty, Format, Major(s), Location and Link also work), and extra columns are ignored. A single combined CSV with every column also works: shared details move to the Faculty tab automatically.

Add it to OpenLab

  1. Open your page in the WordPress editor and add a Custom HTML block (+ → Custom HTML).
  2. Paste the static version’s code into the block.
  3. Click Preview, then Update. Next semester, load the new workbook here and replace the block's code.

Everything runs in your browser: your files are never uploaded anywhere.
